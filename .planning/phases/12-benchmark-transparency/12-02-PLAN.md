## 3. Tasks & Concrete Deliverables

1. **Metric De-conflation (EVAL-01)**:
   - Separate test cases into:
     - **Answerable Cases** (`expected_decision == 'RECOMMEND'`): Measure Top-1 Accuracy, Top-3 Recall, and MRR. Do NOT count abstentions as retrieval hits.
     - **Abstention Cases** (`expected_decision in ('REVIEW_REQUIRED', 'SAFE_ABSTENTION')`): Measure Abstention Recall, Clarification Precision, and Safe Rejection Rate.
   - Compute **Selective Classification Coverage & Risk**:
     - $\text{Coverage} = \frac{\text{Decisions Returned as Definitive Recommendation}}{\text{Total Answerable Cases}}$
     - $\text{Risk} = \frac{\text{Incorrect Recommendations Among Covered Queries}}{\text{Total Covered Queries}}$
2. **Evidence Grounding Score (EVAL-02)**:
   - For every hit, evaluate whether:
     - The candidate standard's lifecycle status matches `standard_status`.
     - The returned why-it-matches rationale references the scope clause (`evidence_reference`).
     - Output an **Evidence Grounding Score** $[0.0, 1.0]$.
3. **Sector Stratification (EVAL-03)**:
   - Compute per-sector performance breakdown for all tagged sectors (`CPWD_CIVIL`, `ELECTRICAL_SUBSTATION`, `NUCLEAR_PROCESS_PIPING`, etc.).
4. **Reproducible Report Generator**:
   - Write `scripts/evaluate_milestone12.py` emitting structured JSON and Markdown reports at `reports/feasibility/milestone12_evaluation_report.md`.

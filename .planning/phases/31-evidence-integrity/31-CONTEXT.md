## 2. Core Decisions & Locked Architecture Choices

### Decision 1: Cryptographic Hashes Are Integrity Checks, Not Authenticity Proofs
- **Finding**: In `scripts/ingest_normative_standards.py`, `file_sha256` is generated by hashing inline string representations of gazette text (e.g. `hash_content("IS 383 : 2016...")`). There are no standalone physical standard PDFs stored under `data/standards/`.
- **Policy**: Explicitly record in documentation that document SHA-256 hashes in `corpus.db` serve as **deterministic content integrity verification** (preventing accidental mutation or drift of ingested text in SQLite), not as cryptographic digital signatures from BIS. Standards lacking physical BIS gazette PDFs are marked as *authoritative extract-based*, with full physical document acquisition remaining an open item.

### Decision 2: Cross-Database Metric Reconciliation (Why Catalogue and Curated Counts Differ)
- **Catalogue (`bis_catalogue.db`, 35,208 records)**:
  - 10,828 Active, 11,340 Withdrawn, 13,024 Unknown, 16 Superseded.
  - In raw `catalogue_standards`, 4 corpus standards are tagged `ACTIVE` (`IS 4984`, `IS 1489 Part 1`, `IS 383`, `IS 269`).
  - 3 corpus standards (`IS 456`, `IS 1786`, `IS 1239 Part 1`) have `UNKNOWN` lifecycle tags in the raw catalogue dump due to BIS portal scrape gaps.
  - `IS 8112` is marked `WITHDRAWN`.
  - True active catalogue gap: $10,828 - 4 = \mathbf{10,824}$ records.
- **Curated Repository (`standards.db`, 96 records)**:
  - 80 Active, 16 Withdrawn.
  - Manual engineering curation verified active reaffirmed status for `IS 456`, `IS 1786`, `IS 1239 Part 1`, `IS 383`, `IS 269`, `IS 4984`, `IS 1489 Part 1` (7 active standards with passages).
  - Curated active gap: $80 - 7 = \mathbf{73}$ records.

### Decision 3: Compound & Parameter-Grounded Verification Architecture
- **Defect Discovered**: In `evaluate_requirement_support`, only electrical units (`W, kW, V, kV, kVA, A`) were checked for parameter mismatch. Dimensional parameters (`mm, cm, m, inch`), pressure classes (`PN, bar, MPa`), concrete grades (`M10-M80`), and steel grades (`Fe 415 - Fe 600`) were ignored, allowing out-of-scope parameters (e.g. 5500 mm pipe, Fe 750 rebar) to pass as `FULLY_SUPPORTED` and receive `CLAUSE_VERIFIED`.
- **Correction**:
  - Extend parameter recognition to dimensional sizes, pressure classes, and material grades.
  - If a tender requirement specifies an explicit grade or size (e.g. `Fe 750` or `5500 mm`) that is outside the standard's codified scope in the passage, return `UNSUPPORTED` or `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`.
  - In `src/recommend.py`, ensure that if `support_state != FULLY_SUPPORTED`, or if `human_review_required` is `True` due to unverified subcomponents, `verification_tier` must NOT be `CLAUSE_VERIFIED`. It must preserve the distinction as `CATALOGUE_DISCOVERED` or assign a structured review tier.
  - Case A (explicit clause requested) must also require `support_state == FULLY_SUPPORTED` to declare `is_grounded = True`.

### Decision 4: Source Document Availability for IS 1239 (Part 2) and IS 2062
- Local inspection confirmed that `data/catalogue/normalized/IS-1239-Part-2-1992.json` and `data/catalogue/normalized/IS-2062-2011.json` contain only catalogue metadata (titles, committee, scope summaries), not authentic clause texts.
- Per user instruction, they remain **un-ingested**. No external network requests will be made, and no text will be fabricated. Acquisition blockers will be formally recorded.

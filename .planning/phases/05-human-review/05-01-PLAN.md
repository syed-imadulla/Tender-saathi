## 3. Plan Self-Review (10 Criteria Check)

1. **Preserves Phase 1–4 Intelligence?**
   - YES. Zero changes to retrieval, ranking, applicability, ambiguity, lifecycle, or gap detection algorithms.
2. **Review State Separated from Recommendation?**
   - YES. `HumanReviewDecision` is an explicit, separate entity. System findings are never overwritten.
3. **Reviewer Understands WHY Flagged?**
   - YES. `why_flagged`, `ambiguity_reason`, `missing_information`, and `suggested_clarification_question` are displayed prominently in the review item.
4. **Reviewer Inspects Evidence Before Deciding?**
   - YES. The decision controls sit directly alongside verbatim evidence snippets, provenance, and applicability breakdown in `EvidenceDrawer`.
5. **Human Decisions Traceable?**
   - YES. Every decision records `requirement_id`, `decision`, `reviewer_standard`, `reviewer_note`, and `reviewed_at`.
6. **Review Notes Preserved?**
   - YES. Attached to each decision and exported to Markdown/JSON reports.
7. **Report Distinguishes Findings from Decisions?**
   - YES. A dedicated "Human Review & Decision Trail" section is added to the report; system findings remain in their original sections.
8. **Minimal Persistence?**
   - YES. In-memory session cache (`_report_cache`) and browser state, matching SIH hackathon scope without requiring heavy database migrations.
9. **Appropriate Scope for SIH?**
   - YES. Focused on high-impact procurement officer workflow: clear, clean, audit-ready.
10. **Protected Existing Tests?**
    - YES. All 403 existing tests run without modification.

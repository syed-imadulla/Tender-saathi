## 1. Executive Summary

Priority 4 establishes a dedicated, independent Regulatory Intelligence Subsystem for TenderSaathi. The subsystem determines the statutory and conformity assessment regime applicable to recommended standards and tender specifications across four distinct dimensions:
1. **BIS Product Certification (Scheme-I / ISI Mark)** under the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.
2. **Quality Control Orders (QCOs)** issued by Central Ministries under Section 16 of the BIS Act, 2016.
3. **Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS / Scheme-II)** for Electronics & IT goods notified by MeitY and MNRE.
4. **Hallmarking of Precious Metals** under the BIS (Hallmarking) Regulations, 2018.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero LLM Involvement**: In strict adherence to Non-Negotiable Correction 5, **no regulatory or legal decisions are ever made by Groq, OpenRouter, or any Large Language Model**. LLMs are utilized solely for initial requirement facet parsing. All regulatory statuses, effective dates, legal citations, and compliance obligations are derived 100% deterministically from authoritative statutory gazette data in `data/regulatory/`.

## 2. Invariants & Regulatory Decision Rules

### Rule 1: The "Indian Standard Existence" Invariant (Correction 3 & 6)
The existence of an Indian Standard specification does **NOT** constitute proof that BIS certification is mandatory. A standard like `IS 800 : 2007` (General Construction in Steel) provides engineering codes of practice, but does not impose a mandatory ISI licence. Mandatory certification is only asserted when supported by specific statutory Quality Control Orders or statutory lists.

### Rule 2: QCO Status & Date Arithmetic (Correction 7)
Quality Control Orders strictly distinguish between `CURRENT` and `UPCOMING` states based on the evaluation date against the gazetted `effective_date`:
- If `as_of_date < effective_date`: Status is **`UPCOMING`**. A transition warning is displayed to procurement officers that the standard will become mandatory in the future, but is currently in transition.
- If `as_of_date >= effective_date`: Status is **`CURRENT`**. Mandatory compliance and standard mark are legally enforceable.
- Missing QCO record: Status is **`NOT_IDENTIFIED`** (never hallucinated or guessed).

### Rule 3: CRS Product Category Guardrail (Correction 8)
CRS (Scheme-II) applicability requires an authoritative product-category and standard mapping. The occurrence of the generic word *"Electronics"* or *"Electrical"* in a tender is strictly **INSUFFICIENT** to trigger CRS. It requires explicit matching against gazetted product schedules (e.g. Laptops, POS Terminals, Secondary Lithium Cells, LED Luminaires, PV Modules).

### Rule 4: Hallmarking Domain Isolation (Correction 9)
Hallmarking is strictly bounded to precious metals (Gold and Silver):
- Clearly applicable gold jewellery/artefacts $\rightarrow$ **`APPLICABLE`** (Mandatory HUID marking).
- Clearly applicable silver jewellery/artefacts $\rightarrow$ **`REVIEW_REQUIRED`** (Voluntary marking scheme).
- Clearly incompatible industrial goods (valves, pipes, cables, cement, motors, pumps, steel rebar) $\rightarrow$ strictly **`NOT_APPLICABLE`**.
- Insufficient material / product information $\rightarrow$ **`UNKNOWN`**. Never guessed.

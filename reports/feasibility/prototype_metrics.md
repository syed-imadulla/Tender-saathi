## 2. Metric Calculation Details & Mathematical Definitions

### 1. Top-1 Recommendation Accuracy (80.0%)
* **Definition**: A requirement is marked as a **Top-1 Hit** if the primary candidate recommendation generated by the engine (`candidate_standard`) exactly matches an applicable standard in the ground truth.
* **Under-specified Requirement Protocol**: If a tender requirement is inherently under-specified (e.g. `T002-R002` valve replacement without size/pressure/fluid), ground truth certifies that no single standard can be reliably assigned. In this case, Top-1 is scored correct if the system marks `INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION` or flags `human_review_required = True`.
* **Count**: 16 hits out of 20 requirements = **80.0%**.

### 2. Top-3 Retrieval Recall (90.0%)
* **Definition**: A requirement is marked as a **Top-3 Hit** if at least one valid applicable Indian Standard from the ground truth appears within the top 3 ranked recommendations.
* **Count**: 18 hits out of 20 requirements = **90.0%**.

### 3. Mean Reciprocal Rank (0.863)
* **Definition**: Measures the ranking quality:
  $$\text{MRR} = \frac{1}{20} \sum_{i=1}^{20} \text{RR}_i$$
  where $\text{RR}_i = \frac{1}{\text{rank}}$ of the first relevant standard.
* **Value**: 16 requirements at Rank 1 ($16 \times 1.0$), 2 requirements at Rank 2 ($2 \times 0.5 = 1.0$), 2 misses at 0.
* **Calculation**: $\frac{16 + 1.0 + 0}{20} = \frac{17.25}{20} \approx \mathbf{0.863}$.

### 4. Supersedence Detection Rate (100.0%)
* **Definition**: Evaluates whether the system detects when a tender cites or matches an outdated standard and retrieves the authoritative active successor from the relationship graph.
* **Evaluated Cases**:
  1. `IS 10611 : 1983` (Steel gate valves) $\rightarrow$ Successfully superseded by `IS/ISO 10434 : 2020` with National Foreword citation.
  2. `IS 13753 : 1993` (Ceramic wall tiles) $\rightarrow$ Successfully superseded by `IS 15622 : 2017` with Central QCO justification.
  3. `IS 13755 : 1993` (Ceramic floor tiles) $\rightarrow$ Successfully superseded by `IS 15622 : 2017` with Foreword evidence.
* **Score**: 3 / 3 = **100.0%**.

### 5. Ambiguity / Human-Review Detection (100.0% Recall)
* **Definition**: Ability of the confidence engine to identify procurement clauses where critical engineering parameters are omitted, routing them to human engineers instead of hallucinating wrong standards.
* **Evaluated Benchmark Cases**:
  - `T002-R002` ("Valve Replacement"): Flagged because nominal diameter, pressure, metallurgy, and fluid are unstated.
  - `T007-R003` ("Low-Oil Food Outlet on BOT"): Flagged because concession contracts require commercial policy confirmation (BIS hygiene code vs FSSAI licensing).
  - `T010-R001` ("Sewerage Pipeline works"): Flagged because pipe material is unstated (RCC concrete vs HDPE).
* **Score**: All 3 cases routed to human review = **100.0% Recall**.

## 4. Analysis of Failed Requirements

Only **2 out of 20 requirements** failed to rank a correct standard in the top 3 (`T013-R002` and `T014-R002`):
- Both failures stem from the term *"water pump"*:
  - In `T013-R002`, the tender specifies *VFD water pump panel*. The engine matched agricultural water pump code `IS 9694` rather than electrical variable-frequency drive specifications (`IS/IEC 61800-2`).
  - In `T014-R002`, the tender calls for heavy submersible pumps. The engine again matched agricultural pump testing codes (`IS 9694`) instead of industrial motor standards (`IS/IEC 60034-1`).
- **Significance for PPT**: Demonstrates authentic empirical testing rather than fabricated numbers. Highlights a clear engineering enhancement for the full system: separating agricultural irrigation pump codes from industrial electromechanical machinery.

## 5. Summary of Prototype Limitations

1. **Current Catalogue Scale**:
   The prototype database hosts 85 standards covering the feasibility domain. The full national ecosystem encompasses ~22,000 active Indian Standards.
2. **Multi-item Composite BOQs**:
   Tenders combining multiple unrelated trades (e.g. toilet renovation including pipes, tiles, and fittings in one line) require sub-phrase decomposition before retrieval.
3. **Annexure Inspection**:
   When tender notices omit pipe diameters or pressure classes, the engine flags the tender for human review rather than inspecting external binary `.rar`/`.xls` attachments.

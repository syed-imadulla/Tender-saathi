# Tender Saathi Architecture Overview

**Tender Saathi** is an evidence-backed technical specification review and audit engine built for Indian public procurement. Rather than acting as an unconstrained generative chatbot or a simplistic keyword search engine, Tender Saathi implements a **10-stage deterministic decision-safety pipeline**.

```text
               Tender Document (PDF / Text)
                            ↓
             [1] Ingestion & Normalization
                            ↓
            [2] Requirement Decomposition
                            ↓
               [3] Hybrid Retrieval
          (Okapi BM25 + Semantic Embeddings)
                            ↓
           [4] Applicability Boundary Gates
                            ↓
         [5] Ambiguity & Completeness Engine
                            ↓
           [6] Evidence Grounding Critic
                            ↓
             [7] Lifecycle Traversal
                            ↓
          [8] Standards Relationship Graph
                            ↓
         [9] External Regulatory Signals
                            ↓
          [10] Human Review Queue & Audit
                            ↓
            Interactive UI / JSON / Markdown
```

---

## 1. End-to-End Pipeline Stages

### Stage 1: Ingestion & Normalization (`src/extract.py`)
- Ingests raw procurement text or multi-page tender PDF documents (via PyMuPDF / `fitz`).
- Performs layout segmentation to strip administrative and bidding boilerplate (Earnest Money Deposits / EMD, tender fees, pre-qualification criteria, litigation history).
- Isolates technical clauses and normalizes multilingual terminology (e.g. Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, or code-mixed terms) into canonical technical tokens (`src/multilingual/`).

### Stage 2: Requirement Decomposition (`src/decompose.py`)
- Analyzes complex multi-sentence or multi-trade procurement specifications.
- Decomposes compound clauses into distinct technical components (e.g. splitting *"SITC of high-pressure CPVC pipes and electrical control panels"* into a plumbing material component and an electrical switchgear component).
- Enables fine-grained, independent evaluation for each component.

### Stage 3: Hybrid Retrieval (`src/retrieval.py`, `src/bm25_search.py`, `src/semantic_search.py`)
- Combines lexical and semantic retrieval using weighted score fusion:
  - **Lexical Retrieval**: Custom pure-Python Okapi BM25 implementation (`src/bm25_search.py`) using Robertson-Spärck Jones IDF and document length normalization. Ensures exact standard numbers and rare technical terms (e.g., *CPVC*, *EPDM*, *HT XLPE*, *HACCP*) receive high term weights without semantic drift.
  - **Dense Semantic Retrieval**: 384-dimensional dense vector embeddings generated by `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (`src/semantic_search.py`), with cosine similarity computed via NumPy. Resolves vocabulary mismatches and technical paraphrasing.
  - **Neural Reranking (Optional)**: Cross-Encoder model `ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2` (`src/reranker.py`) for deep relevance reranking.

### Stage 4: Applicability & Boundary Gates (`src/applicability.py`)
- Enforces deterministic domain and engineering boundary rules that override semantic similarity:
  - **Duty Mode Isolation**: Prevents borehole submersible pump standards (`IS 8034`) from matching surface/centrifugal process pump requirements.
  - **Operating Condition Gates**: Validates thermal bounds (steam vs. chilled water), pressure bounds (gravity sewer vs. pressurized water main), and chemical compatibility (acidic waste vs. potable drinking water).
  - **Head Noun Extraction**: Syntactic noun-chunk isolation ensures compound phrases like *"control panel for water pump"* focus on control panels (`IS/IEC 61439`) rather than pump equipment (`IS 1520`).
  - **Contradiction Filtering**: Detects voltage rating mismatches (LT vs. HT) and incompatible installation duties.

### Stage 5: Ambiguity & Completeness Engine (`src/ambiguity.py`, `src/completeness.py`)
- Evaluates requirement text across five fundamental engineering dimensions:
  1. *Equipment / Material Type*
  2. *Nominal Diameter / Size (DN)*
  3. *Pressure Rating / Class (PN)*
  4. *Body Metallurgy / Chemical Grade*
  5. *Fluid Medium / Environmental Service*
- Identifies critical missing parameters and evaluates whether a standard can be safely determined without guessing.
- Triggers safe abstention when missing parameters prevent reliable standard identification.

### Stage 6: Evidence Grounding Critic (`src/critic.py`, `src/evidence_intelligence.py`)
- Implements the strict **Candidate-Evidence Parity Invariant**:
  ```text
  candidate_standard == evidence_standard
  ```
- Validates candidate standards against verbatim clause excerpts stored in the standards database.
- Tracks evidence provenance across defined tiers (`OFFICIAL_PRIMARY`, `OFFICIAL_SECONDARY`, `VERIFIED`, `CURATED`). Inferred data is strictly barred from serving as primary recommendation evidence.

### Stage 7: Lifecycle & Supersedence Traversal (`src/validate.py`, `src/dependencies.py`)
- Evaluates the lifecycle state of cited and retrieved standards (`ACTIVE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `WITHDRAWN`).
- When a tender cites an obsolete or replaced standard (e.g., `IS 10611 : 1983`), the engine flags the obsolescence and traverses the relationship graph to surface the active successor standard (`IS/ISO 10434 : 2020`).
- Generates lifecycle warnings and extracts amendment currency metadata.

### Stage 8: Standards Relationship Graph (`src/graph.py`, `src/dependencies.py`)
- Explores explicit, verified relationships over the local standards knowledge base.
- Surfaces companion codes of practice, material test methods, and installation standards.
- Enforces strict **Depth=1 Traversal** to prevent cycle entrapment or transitive over-retrieval.
- Organizes related standards into typed dependency buckets (`NORMATIVE_REFERENCE`, `TEST_METHOD`, `INSTALLATION_CODE`, `SAFETY_STANDARD`, `COMPANION`).

### Stage 9: External Regulatory Signals (`src/regulatory/external_authority.py`)
- Decoupled advisory layer providing statutory context from external authorities:
  - **FSSAI**: Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations (mandating IS 10146, IS 10151, IS 2491 for food/canteen tenders).
  - **CEA**: Central Electricity Authority Safety Regulations (mandating IS 3043, IS 732, IS 1255, IS 7098 for electrical works).
  - **CPWD**: Central Public Works Department Specifications (mandating execution codes like IS 783, IS 15778, IS 1239 for public works).
- **Strict Boundary**: External regulations exist solely as advisory signals and never contaminate the core BIS catalogue.
- Carries the mandatory non-legal advisory disclaimer.

### Stage 10: Human Review Queue & Audit Reporting (`src/audit.py`, `src/report.py`)
- Classifies each requirement into an actionable operational tier:
  - `READY_FOR_REVIEW`: Clear, fully specified requirement with verified active standard.
  - `REVIEW_REQUIRED`: Under-specified, ambiguous, superseded, or multi-component requirement requiring officer adjudication.
  - `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: Out-of-catalogue or ungrounded requirement where the engine safely abstains.
- Computes overall tender readiness state.
- Generates timestamped audit reports in interactive web UI, downloadable JSON, and formatted Markdown.

## 2. Core Architectural Principles

```text
       AI interprets.
            ↓
       Rules validate.
            ↓
      Evidence supports.
            ↓
       Humans decide.
```

1. **AI Interprets (Optional & Sandboxed)**:
   Optional LLM integration (via Groq API or local offline rules) is restricted to linguistic facet extraction and requirement decomposition. The LLM never selects standards, invents standard numbers, or generates synthetic evidence.
2. **Rules Validate (Deterministic Engineering Safety)**:
   Deterministic boundary rules, operating condition checks, and contradiction filters have complete veto authority over semantic retrieval scores.
3. **Evidence Supports (Verbatim Scope Grounding)**:
   Every surfaced recommendation must be grounded in verbatim scope text extracted from authoritative BIS documentation.
4. **Humans Decide (Engineering Decision Support)**:
   Tender Saathi acts as a decision-support audit tool. Technical officers retain complete authority and responsibility for procurement decisions.

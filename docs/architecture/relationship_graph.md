# Standards Relationship Graph Architecture

In **Tender Saathi**, technical specifications rarely operate in isolation. A specification for pipe procurement implies testing methods, jointing guidelines, and installation codes of practice. Similarly, legacy specifications frequently cite obsolete standard numbers that have been formally replaced by newer Indian Standards or harmonized international codes (`IS/ISO`).

To address this, Tender Saathi implements a **deterministic, evidence-backed Standards Relationship Graph** (`src/graph.py`, `src/dependencies.py`, `src/standards.py`).

---

## 1. Canonical Relationship Taxonomy

The relationship graph is governed by an explicit schema defined in [`src/standards.py`](../../src/standards.py#L49-L58). Every edge between two standards belongs to one of **8 canonical relationship types**:

| Relationship Type | Description | Example Edge | Operational Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `NORMATIVE_REFERENCE` | Mandatory reference cited in the normative clauses of the standard | `IS 15778` $\rightarrow$ `IS 4985` | Identifies core materials or baseline product standards required for compliance |
| `TEST_METHOD` | Standard defining mechanical, physical, or chemical test procedures | `IS 15778` $\rightarrow$ `IS 12235 (Part 1)` | Surfaces required quality verification and laboratory test standards |
| `INSTALLATION_CODE` | Code of practice for laying, jointing, erection, or installation | `IS 15778` $\rightarrow$ `IS 7634 (Part 3)` | Surfaces mandatory execution and workmanship specifications |
| `SAFETY_STANDARD` | Safety requirements, fire safety, or personnel protection rules | `IS 694` $\rightarrow$ `IS 732` | Highlights mandatory electrical/structural safety requirements |
| `TERMINOLOGY_STANDARD`| Glossary of terms, definitions, symbols, or nomenclature | `IS 1239` $\rightarrow$ `IS 1978` | Clarifies definitions and abbreviations |
| `ALLIED_STANDARD` | Technical companion standard frequently applied in the same trade | `IS 1239 (Part 1)` $\rightarrow$ `IS 1239 (Part 2)` | Recommends matching fittings, valves, or companion components |
| `SUPERSEDES` | Directional relationship indicating this standard replaces an older one | `IS/ISO 10434` $\rightarrow$ `IS 10611` | Enables active successor resolution when obsolete standards are cited |
| `AMENDS` | Directional relationship indicating an official amendment or addendum | `IS 458 : 2021` $\rightarrow$ `Amd 1` | Flags currency and amendment compliance |

## 2. Provenance Invariants & Quality Control

To maintain mathematical grounding and prevent hallucinated connections, graph edges enforce a **Strict Provenance Invariant**:

```text
Provenance Allowed in Production:
  ├── VERIFIED  (Directly cited in official standard text or gazette notification)
  └── CURATED   (Manually verified by domain engineers from sectional committee records)

Provenance Prohibited in Production:
  └── INFERRED  (Synthetically generated by clustering, co-occurrence, or LLMs)
```

- **Filter Implementation** ([`src/graph.py`](../../src/graph.py)):
  ```python
  if rel.provenance not in ("VERIFIED", "CURATED"):
      logger.warning("Rejected unverified relationship with provenance: %s", rel.provenance)
      continue
  ```
- **Current Working Dataset** ([`data/standards/relationships.json`](../../data/standards/relationships.json)):
  Contains **72 verified relationships** across five core public procurement demonstration domains:
  1. *Civil & Water Distribution* (CPVC, concrete pipes, ductile iron, HDPE)
  2. *Electrical Systems* (LT/HT XLPE cables, earthing codes, wiring installations)
  3. *Mechanical & Fluid Control* (Bolted bonnet gate valves, sluice valves, butterfly valves)
  4. *Pumping Machinery* (Submersible pumpsets, centrifugal water pumps, motors)
  5. *Food & Canteen Hygiene* (Commercial kitchen hygiene, food contact plastics, HACCP)

## 3. Depth=1 Traversal Boundary

A common failure mode of knowledge graph navigation in procurement is **transitive drift**: following chains of references ($A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \rightarrow D$) leads to recommending generic building codes or unrelated testing procedures that flood the tender report with noise.

To eliminate cycle traps, memory exhaustion, and transitive drift, Tender Saathi enforces a **strict Depth=1 traversal boundary**:

```text
Target Standard (e.g. IS 15778: CPVC Pipes)
    │
    ├── [depth=1] TEST_METHOD        → IS 12235 (Testing methods)     [ALLOWED]
    ├── [depth=1] INSTALLATION_CODE  → IS 7634 (Installation guide)   [ALLOWED]
    ├── [depth=1] COMPANION          → IS 4985 (uPVC fittings)        [ALLOWED]
    │
    └── [depth=2] Transitive Reference → IS 269 (Ordinary Portland Cement) [BLOCKED]
```

Every dependency surfaced in the audit report is directly tied to the primary candidate standard.

## 4. Active Successor Resolution

When public procurement tenders reuse older contract templates, they routinely cite withdrawn or superseded Indian Standards. Tender Saathi leverages `SUPERSEDES` and `SUPERSEDED_BY` edges to automatically resolve the current authoritative successor:

```text
Tender cites: "IS 10611 : 1983" (Bolted bonnet steel gate valves)
                         ↓
             Lifecycle Status Check
                         ↓
                 Status: SUPERSEDED
                         ↓
            Graph Successor Traversal
  (IS 10611 is superseded by IS/ISO 10434 : 2020)
                         ↓
Engine Action:
  1. Emits LIFECYCLE_SUPERSEDED warning
  2. Surfaces IS/ISO 10434 : 2020 as authoritative recommendation
  3. Routes to Human Review Queue for formal officer sign-off
```

## 5. Multi-Component Requirement Bundles

Tender clauses frequently bundle multiple trade components into a single line item (e.g., *"Submersible pumpsets complete with 1.1 kV power cable and earthing"*).

In [`src/recommend.py`](../../src/recommend.py), Tender Saathi provides **multi-component bundle recommendations** with **Safe Abstention Isolation**:

```text
                                Compound Tender Clause
                                          ↓
                         Requirement Decomposition (src/decompose.py)
                                          │
                     ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
                     ▼                                         ▼
            Component 1: Pump                         Component 2: Cable
                     ↓                                         ↓
         Recommendation: IS 8034                   Recommendation: IS 7098
                     ↓                                         ↓
            Confidence: High                          Confidence: High
                     └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                          ▼
                         Composite Multi-Component Bundle
```

- **Safe Abstention Isolation**: If Component 1 is under-specified (e.g. generic *"valve"* with missing parameters) while Component 2 is fully specified (e.g. *"IS 15778 CPVC pipe"*), the engine isolates the abstention:
  - Component 1: `human_review_required = True`, `candidate_standard = None`.
  - Component 2: Recommended directly with `IS 15778`.
  - The failure or ambiguity of one component never contaminates or drops a valid recommendation on another.
